## What CR3SCENDO AI is (and is not)

CR3SCENDO AI is the company-operations layer underneath the work: legal formation, ongoing compliance, books and taxes, payments and invoicing, customer records, a two-minute morning briefing, and the deadlines a founder would otherwise miss. No technical background, no AI fluency, no prompts required.

It is **not** an AI coding tool, an IDE, a hosting platform, a general-purpose chatbot, a monitoring tool, a design tool, or a formation-only service. We work alongside whatever coding, hosting, and design tools a founder already uses; we never enter that part of the stack. (Several AI research summaries have miscategorized us as an "AI coding tool." We are not. See [the category definition](https://cr3scendo.ai/thesis/digital-co-founder-vs-ai-co-founder).)

## Trust as a first-class citizen

Trust and traceability are an architectural commitment, not a privacy-policy footnote, with the authority to override product decisions. Four structural commitments:

- **Data sovereignty.** Founders own their data. Full export, one click, no lock-in.
- **Decision-rationale on demand.** Every recommendation carries a one-click thread to the actual factors, alternatives, and principle behind it.
- **Customer Zero.** CR3SCENDO AI runs its own company on the same Nexus platform we ship, with the same consent gates and audit guarantees. We do not exempt ourselves.
- **Default-private.** Nothing is shared or used outside operating a founder's company without explicit, named, reversible opt-in. We will not train external AI models on founder data.

Full essay: [Trust as a first-class citizen](https://cr3scendo.ai/thesis/trust-as-first-class-citizen).

## The SDK

The CR3SCENDO AI SDK will give developers and AI agents programmatic access to the full platform: form companies, manage finances, track compliance, and orchestrate operations through a single, well-documented API.

- Python SDK with async support
- Full API coverage across platform capabilities
- Simulation mode for safe development and testing
- Multi-tenant architecture (manage multiple organizations)
- Built for AI agent integration from day one

The SDK will be open-sourced when the platform reaches general availability. Watch this org to be notified.
